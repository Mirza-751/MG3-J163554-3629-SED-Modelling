NOTES:
- 'Published' = Sanchez Zaballa+2025 one-zone best-fit (Table 2, eta=0.083)
- 'This work' Zone 1 = compact zone (between BLR and DT), Sync+SSC+EC
- This work additionally includes a Zone 2 (extended, beyond DT,
  Sync+SSC only) which fits the radio data points not modeled
  in the published one-zone work, plus the NuSTAR hard X-ray points.
- Differences in B, BulkFactor, N, gamma_break, p, p_1 reflect the
  redistribution of the emission budget across two zones and the
  inclusion of additional (radio + NuSTAR) data.
- Central-engine quantities (M_BH, L_Disk, accr_eff, R_DT, T_DT,
  R_BLR_in/out) agree well between the two works, anchored by the
  same accretion-disk thermal bump in the optical/UV data.
